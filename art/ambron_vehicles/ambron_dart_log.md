# ambron_dart — Production Log

## Config
- Type: light air unit (interceptor), Ambron, game asset for Shardfall (Godot 4.7, Mobile renderer)
- Target: assets/models/ambron_dart.glb (uncompressed), assets/icons/ambron_dart.png
- Budget: 4-8k triangles, radius 0.6, about 1.3 m wingspan, about 0.6 m thick (docs/ART_SPEC.md)
- Style: Ambron, chunky toy-like sci-fi in the family of the harvester drone
- Mode: auto (brief delegated 2026-11-14); scripted build, review renders by EEVEE in Blender 5.2

## Reference
- art/ref/ambron_style_reference.png (Alex's Tripo harvester drone turnaround) and docs/ART_SPEC.md
- Brief: "fast light interceptor: small sleek rounded body, forward-swept little wings, twin glowing engine pods, twin nose cannons"
- Read from the drone: rounded cream shell plates over graphite mechanics, gold trim and fins, cyan lenses
  set in graphite bezels, no grime, soft bevels; the camera looks down at 55 degrees from 18-45 m, so
  colour blocking and silhouette matter more than detail.

## Intent
- Palette (ART_SPEC Ambron look): cream #EDE3CF (shade #D6C9AE), gold #E0A33A (dark #B9771E), graphite
  #2B2F36 (light #474D57), near-black #17191E for track and tube interiors; `team` light grey #C9CDD3;
  `glow_cyan` #5FE3FF with emission 2.2
- Materials: `ambron_body` (a 64 px palette texture, one 16 px cell per colour, UVs at the cell centres,
  nearest filtering, plus a matching metallic/roughness texture: roughness 0.38-0.5, metallic 0-0.3 on
  gold and graphite), `team`, `glow_cyan`. Principled BSDF only; all single-sided.
- Family cues from the drone: the graphite face plate with a cyan pill lens, the big side discs (cream rim,
  graphite bezel, domed cyan lens), the two gold "ear" fins, big soft bevels (3 segments) everywhere.

## Inventory
- Fuselage: lathed cream teardrop, flattened to 84 % in height, with a gold spine strip
- Canopy: graphite frame with a cyan glass dome on the nose (the drone face)
- Forward-swept cream wings with a little dihedral, a flush `team` band near each tip, gold tips with a cyan
  running light, gold canards by the nose
- Twin engine pods: cream with a gold band, a graphite intake ring, a graphite nozzle round a cyan exhaust,
  and the drone's gold ear fins on top (canted out 16 degrees); a cyan core in the tail between them
- Twin nose cannons under the chin: graphite barrels, gold rings, cyan glowing tips; a graphite keel

## Pipeline
- Build (art/ambron_vehicles/build.py, scripted bmesh): rounded boxes (bevelled, 2-3 segments), YZ-profile
  prisms for hulls and turrets, lathed parts for nozzles, wheels, lenses, ducts and barrels; each part UV'd
  onto its palette cell, parts joined per object, smooth shading with edges over 42 degrees kept sharp, a
  weighted-normal (face area) pass applied.
- Settle: lowest point to z = 0 and the bounding box centred in X and Y (baked into the body mesh, so its
  origin is the ground centre).
- Result: 4,404 triangles (body 4,404); 1.284 x 1.33 x 0.525 m (X x Y x Z); front -Y.
- Objects: `body` (origin 0, 0, 0); Empty `muzzle` (no parent) at (0, -0.68, 0.09).
- Export: GLB, selection only, modifiers applied, +Y up, no cameras, lights or animations; glow materials
  carry KHR_materials_emissive_strength; 139 KB.
- Icon: assets/icons/ambron_dart.png, 256 px, transparent, EEVEE, three-quarter from the front-left (camera at
  -X, -Y) 24 degrees up, warm key, cool rim, framed tight.

## Review rounds
- Renders per round in art/ambron_vehicles/review/round<N>/: front three-quarter, back three-quarter, the
  game camera (55 degrees down from 25 m, 45 degree vertical field of view, 512 px, on a ground plane; air units lifted to 3.5 m),
  and (final round only) a sheet of all four with the icon.
- Round 1 (review/round1): reads as an Ambron fighter. Problems: 2,828 triangles (under the 4k floor);
  0.42 m thick; the gold wingtip blocks hung below the wing like pods; the `team` band read as a clip;
  the body's origin was moved by settling (origin not at 0, 0, 0).
- Round 2 (review/round2): denser fuselage and pod profiles, canards, keel, tail core, taller fins (4,404
  triangles, 0.53 m thick); gold tips and team band cut from the wing outline so they sit flush; the settle
  offset baked into the mesh (origin at the ground centre). New problem: the wingtip lights floated below the
  wing (they missed the dihedral rotation). The game view now lifts air units to 3.5 m.
- Round 3 (review/round3): running lights rotated with the wing. Fixed.
- Round 4 (review/round4): single-sided materials; no holes.
- Reviewer: my own reading of every render each round, against the drone reference and the brief.
