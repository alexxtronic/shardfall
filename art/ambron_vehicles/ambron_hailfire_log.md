# ambron_hailfire — Production Log

## Config
- Type: ground vehicle (missile launcher), Ambron, game asset for Shardfall (Godot 4.7, Mobile renderer)
- Target: assets/models/ambron_hailfire.glb (uncompressed), assets/icons/ambron_hailfire.png
- Budget: 4-8k triangles, radius 0.9, about 2.2 m long and 1.5 m tall (docs/ART_SPEC.md)
- Style: Ambron, chunky toy-like sci-fi in the family of the harvester drone
- Mode: auto (brief delegated 2026-11-14); scripted build, review renders by EEVEE in Blender 5.2

## Reference
- art/ref/ambron_style_reference.png (Alex's Tripo harvester drone turnaround) and docs/ART_SPEC.md
- Brief: "missile launcher: tracked chassis carrying an angled box launcher with a grid of missile tubes (cyan-tipped missiles visible), a separate turret"
- Read from the drone: rounded cream shell plates over graphite mechanics, gold trim and fins, cyan lenses
  set in graphite bezels, no grime, soft bevels; the camera looks down at 55 degrees from 18-45 m, so
  colour blocking and silhouette matter more than detail.

## Intent
- Palette (ART_SPEC Ambron look): cream #EDE3CF (shade #D6C9AE), gold #E0A33A (dark #B9771E), graphite
  #2B2F36 (light #474D57), near-black #17191E for track and tube interiors; `team` light grey #C9CDD3;
  `glow_cyan` #5FE3FF with emission 2.2
- Materials: `ambron_body` (a 64 px palette texture, one 16 px cell per colour, UVs at the cell centres,
  nearest filtering, plus a matching metallic/roughness texture: roughness 0.38-0.5, metallic 0-0.3 on
  gold and graphite), `team`, `glow_cyan`. Principled BSDF only; all single-sided.
- Family cues from the drone: the graphite face plate with a cyan pill lens, the big side discs (cream rim,
  graphite bezel, domed cyan lens), the two gold "ear" fins, big soft bevels (3 segments) everywhere.

## Inventory
- Two tracks: near-black stadium prisms (convex hull of two idler circles and the ground run), four
  graphite road wheels with cream hubcaps on each outer face, a drone side disc as the sprocket at each end
- Cream fenders over the tracks with gold front caps; cream side skirts with a gold lower edge and a `team`
  band
- Chassis: cream prism with the drone face on the glacis and gold glacis strips, graphite belly, rear vent
  plate with three cyan slots
- Turret (separate object): graphite ring, squat cream base, two gold yoke arms with drone side discs at the
  pivot, and the launcher tilted up 24 degrees
- Launcher: cream box, gold front collar, `team` band round its rear, graphite back plate, a graphite front
  face with a 4 x 3 grid of dark tubes, each with a cyan glowing missile tip

## Pipeline
- Build (art/ambron_vehicles/build.py, scripted bmesh): rounded boxes (bevelled, 2-3 segments), YZ-profile
  prisms for hulls and turrets, lathed parts for nozzles, wheels, lenses, ducts and barrels; each part UV'd
  onto its palette cell, parts joined per object, smooth shading with edges over 42 degrees kept sharp, a
  weighted-normal (face area) pass applied.
- Settle: lowest point to z = 0 and the bounding box centred in X and Y (baked into the hull mesh, so its
  origin is the ground centre).
- Result: 7,920 triangles (hull 5,104, turret 2,816); 1.6 x 2.127 x 1.581 m (X x Y x Z); front -Y.
- Objects: `hull` (origin 0, 0, 0); `turret` (origin on its turning axis, at (0, 0.11, 0.62)); Empty `muzzle`, a child of `turret`, local (0, -0.615, 0.81) = world (0, -0.505, 1.43).
- Export: GLB, selection only, modifiers applied, +Y up, no cameras, lights or animations; glow materials
  carry KHR_materials_emissive_strength; 265 KB.
- Icon: assets/icons/ambron_hailfire.png, 256 px, transparent, EEVEE, three-quarter from the front-left (camera at
  -X, -Y) 24 degrees up, warm key, cool rim, framed tight.
