# ambron_assembly (Assembly Bay) — Production Log

## Config
- Type: building (static), Ambron infantry factory (Lancers, Bulwarks), for Shardfall (Godot 4.7, Mobile renderer)
- Target: `assets/models/ambron_assembly.glb` (uncompressed GLB, +Y up, modifiers applied, no cameras, lights or animations) and `assets/icons/ambron_assembly.png` (256 px, transparent)
- Size: 3 x 3 m footprint, built to 2.70 x 2.70 m, 3.19 m tall (spec 3-3.5 m); budget 4-12k triangles
- Style: Ambron (docs/ART_SPEC.md): chunky toy-like sci-fi, cream / gold / graphite, cyan glow, big soft bevels
- Mode: auto (built by an agent, scripted), reviewed by my own reading each round

## Reference
- Image: `art/ref/ambron_style_reference.png` (the Harvester drone turnaround)
- Brief: "Boxy rounded hangar with a large front door (-Y) framed in gold, cyan light strip over the door, robotic crane arm on top."
- Borrowed from the drone: the drone's arms: cream and gold segments on graphite joints with cyan lens caps (the crane), its ear discs (lens pods on both flanks)
- Visual comparison: by eye against the reference sheet and against the other six in a lineup, each round

## Intent
- Palette (ART_SPEC): cream #EDE3CF (tiers #DDD0B6), gold #E0A33A (hazard/braces #B9771E), graphite #2B2F36 (slats #3E444E), cyan glow #5FE3FF; team accents light grey #C8CCD2 (recoloured in game)
- Materials: ambron_body, glow, team. `ambron_body` = one baked colour texture (1024 px) + one metallic-roughness texture (cream rough 0.45, gold rough 0.36 metal 0.25, graphite rough 0.52 metal 0.12); glow materials are emissive (strength 1.4-1.5, dark base colour so the hue holds); `team` is flat light grey
- Finishes: bevels on every block and rim (1-4 segments) with weighted normals; ambient occlusion baked into the colour for panel seams and ground contact (the Mobile renderer has no SSAO); no grime

## Inventory
- graphite plinth (2.7 m), gold lip, cream tier, cyan corner studs
- cream vaulted hangar (squircle arch, 0.17 m bevel), gold arch ribs front and back, a team rib over the middle
- front shutter door: graphite with six lighter slats, gold jambs and lintel, cyan strip on the lintel
- lens pods on both flanks; rear vent with louvres
- roof crane: graphite turret with gold trim, cream yoke, graphite shoulder and elbow joints with cyan caps, cream boom with a gold strip, gold forearm, graphite claw head with a cyan lens and two gold fingers over the front edge (top of the elbow 3.19 m)

## Source
- Method: scripted (Blender 5.2, `art/ambron_buildings/build.py`, function `build_assembly`; shared kit: `base`, `lens_pod`, `ear_fin`, `joint`, `stud`)

## Pipeline
- Scene (headless, `-- ambron_assembly`): 51 parts, each with bevel + weighted-normal modifiers, joined; hidden faces culled (faces on the ground and faces buried inside other parts, found by ray casts).
- Build: 6,250 triangles; 2.70 x 2.70 x 3.19 m; front -Y, origin at the centre of the footprint on the ground (lowest point z = 0).
- Texturing: Smart UV on the cream / gold / graphite faces; Cycles bake of flat colour (1 sample) and AO (160 samples, 1.1 m, with a ground plane); combined as colour x (0.48 + 0.52 AO^0.9); metallic-roughness from the nearest palette colour per texel. Both PNGs packed in the GLB (copies in `cache/`, gitignored).
- Export: `assets/models/ambron_assembly.glb`, 1.02 MB; objects: `ambron_assembly` (6,250 triangles, origin (0.0, 0.0, 0.0) in Blender Z-up, materials ambron_body, glow, team).
- Materials are single-sided (backface culling on).
- Icon: EEVEE, 256 px, transparent film, three-quarter from the front-left and above, warm key + cool rim.
- Review renders: EEVEE 512 px, a three-quarter view and the game camera (55 degrees down, 25 m, 40 degree vertical FOV) on a ground plane with the footprint marked; `lineup_*.png` re-imports all seven GLBs.

## Review rounds
- Round 1 (review/round1/sheet_*.png, flat colours): reads as a hangar; the crane arm was too thin to read from the game camera and the building risked looking like the Forge (both cream boxes with dark doors).
- Round 2 (review/round2, flat): crane thickened (boom 0.22 -> 0.30 m, forearm 0.16 -> 0.22 m), bigger joints and claw, cyan lens on the claw; flank pods slightly larger. 7.2k triangles.
- Round 3 (review/round3, baked, first export, lineup): at game size the rounded roof, team rib and crane separate it from the Forge (square, stacks, hazard brow, orange vents).
- Round 4 (review/round4, final): lighter AO, warmer gold. Nothing blocking.
- Reviewers: my own reading each round (no separate reviewer).
