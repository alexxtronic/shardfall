# ambron_forge (Mech Forge) — Production Log

## Config
- Type: building (static), Ambron vehicle factory (Scorchers, Hailfires), for Shardfall (Godot 4.7, Mobile renderer)
- Target: `assets/models/ambron_forge.glb` (uncompressed GLB, +Y up, modifiers applied, no cameras, lights or animations) and `assets/icons/ambron_forge.png` (256 px, transparent)
- Size: 3 x 3 m footprint, built to 2.70 x 2.70 m, 3.16 m tall (spec 3-3.5 m); budget 4-12k triangles
- Style: Ambron (docs/ART_SPEC.md): chunky toy-like sci-fi, cream / gold / graphite, cyan glow, big soft bevels
- Mode: auto (built by an agent, scripted), reviewed by my own reading each round

## Reference
- Image: `art/ref/ambron_style_reference.png` (the Harvester drone turnaround)
- Brief: "Heavier industrial look: wide front ramp/door, exhaust stacks, gold hazard trim, glowing furnace vents."
- Borrowed from the drone: lens pods on the flanks, the shared plinth, cyan corner lights; the heat is the one warm glow in the set
- Visual comparison: by eye against the reference sheet and against the other six in a lineup, each round

## Intent
- Palette (ART_SPEC): cream #EDE3CF (tiers #DDD0B6), gold #E0A33A (hazard/braces #B9771E), graphite #2B2F36 (slats #3E444E), cyan glow #5FE3FF, furnace glow #FF8A2A; team accents light grey #C8CCD2 (recoloured in game)
- Materials: ambron_body, glow, glow_furnace (orange), team. `ambron_body` = one baked colour texture (1024 px) + one metallic-roughness texture (cream rough 0.45, gold rough 0.36 metal 0.25, graphite rough 0.52 metal 0.12); glow materials are emissive (strength 1.4-1.5, dark base colour so the hue holds); `team` is flat light grey
- Finishes: bevels on every block and rim (1-4 segments) with weighted normals; ambient occlusion baked into the colour for panel seams and ground contact (the Mobile renderer has no SSAO); no grime

## Source
- Method: scripted (Blender 5.2, `art/ambron_buildings/build.py`, function `build_forge`; shared kit: `base`, `lens_pod`, `ear_fin`, `joint`, `stud`)

## Pipeline
- Scene (headless, `-- ambron_forge`): 81 parts, each with bevel + weighted-normal modifiers, joined; hidden faces culled (faces on the ground and faces buried inside other parts, found by ray casts).
- Build: 7,146 triangles; 2.70 x 2.70 x 3.16 m; front -Y, origin at the centre of the footprint on the ground (lowest point z = 0).
- Texturing: Smart UV on the cream / gold / graphite faces; Cycles bake of flat colour (1 sample) and AO (160 samples, 1.1 m, with a ground plane); combined as colour x (0.48 + 0.52 AO^0.9); metallic-roughness from the nearest palette colour per texel. Both PNGs packed in the GLB (copies in `cache/`, gitignored).
- Export: `assets/models/ambron_forge.glb`, 1.03 MB; objects: `ambron_forge` (7,146 triangles, origin (0.0, 0.0, 0.0) in Blender Z-up, materials ambron_body, glow, team, glow_furnace).
- Materials are single-sided (backface culling on).
- Icon: EEVEE, 256 px, transparent film, three-quarter from the front-left and above, warm key + cool rim.
- Review renders: EEVEE 512 px, a three-quarter view and the game camera (55 degrees down, 25 m, 40 degree vertical FOV) on a ground plane with the footprint marked; `lineup_*.png` re-imports all seven GLBs.

## Review rounds

- Round 1 (review/round1/sheet_*.png, flat colours): reads as heavy industry; furnace vents and stack mouths clipped to yellow (emission 3.0).
- Round 2 (review/round2, flat): furnace colour #FF9838 -> #FF8A2A at emission 1.5 with a dark base, now orange; stacks lighter -> 8.0k.
- Round 3 (review/round3, baked, first export, lineup): glowing stack tops read from above at game size.
- Round 4 (review/round4, final): lighter AO, warmer gold. Nothing blocking.
- Reviewers: my own reading each round (no separate reviewer).
