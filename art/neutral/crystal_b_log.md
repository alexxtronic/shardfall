# crystal_b — Production Log

## Config
- Type: resource node (golden crystal cluster, the mineral players harvest), neutral, game asset for Shardfall (Godot 4.7, Mobile renderer)
- Target: assets/models/crystal_b.glb (uncompressed), assets/icons/crystal_b.png
- Budget: 500-1500 triangles, about 2 x 1 m footprint, 1.4-2.2 m tall
- Style: stylised, faceted, readable from the 55 degree game camera at 18-45 m
- Mode: auto (brief delegated 2026-11-14); scripted build, review renders by EEVEE in Blender 5.2

## Reference
- art/ref/ambron_style_reference.png (the yellow crystals in the drone's tray) and docs/ART_SPEC.md
- Brief: "golden spike crystal clusters ... faceted, warm gold #FFC93A to amber #E08A12, bright and saturated, slightly emissive so they glow a little; a cluster of 5-9 sharp hexagonal spikes of different heights leaning outward from a small rocky base"

## Intent
- Palette: crystal gradient by height, amber at the root to gold to pale gold at the tip (wide faces
  #A9530A > #E08A12 > #FFC93A > #FFE07A > #FFF1B8; chamfer faces a step lighter, so every edge catches a
  glint; about a third of the spikes use a deeper amber column); base rock slate #2A303A > #5A6474 with teal
  lichen #2C5F56 / #3E8577
- Materials: `glow_crystal` (the gradient texture as base colour and as emission at 0.45, roughness 0.18,
  flat facets), `rock` (the slate/lichen column texture, roughness 0.85). Principled BSDF only.
- Shape language: hexagonal spikes with chamfered edges (12 faces, wide and narrow), a slight waist, a
  shoulder taper and an off-centre pyramid tip, leaning outward from a collar of dark angular rock.

## Inventory
- 9 spikes in two groups: a 1.6 m spike with three companions on the left, a 1.25 m spike with four smaller ones on the right
- A rocky base of two or three flat angular lumps under the roots and a collar of seven slate rocks round
  them, with teal lichen on some tops; three pebbles on the ground
- Everything below the ground cut away, so the spikes' roots never show and the lowest point is z = 0

## Pipeline
- Build (art/neutral/build.py, scripted bmesh; lathe, transforms, staging, renders and export loaded from
  art/ambron_vehicles/build.py): parts built with their own UVs and material indices, merged into one mesh.
- Result: 936 triangles; 2.085 x 1.07 x 1.645 m (X x Y x Z); lowest point z = 0, centred in X and Y,
  one object `crystal_b` with its origin at the ground centre. Materials: `glow_crystal`, `rock`.
- Export: GLB, selection only, modifiers applied, +Y up, no cameras, lights or animations, single-sided
  materials; 79 KB.
- Icon: assets/icons/crystal_b.png, 256 px, transparent, three-quarter from the front-left.

## Review rounds
- Renders in art/neutral/review/round<N>/: front and back three-quarters, the game camera (55 degrees down
  from 25 m, 45 degree vertical field of view, 512 px, on a ground plane) and, in the final round, a sheet; round 7 adds
  lineup_near/game/far.png (every asset together at 18, 25 and 40 m).
- Round 1 (review/round1): golden, faceted, the chamfer stripes sparkle. Problems: spikes pencil-thin; the
  rocky base all but hidden; heights overshot (crystal_a 2.38 m) because the buried roots tilted below the
  ground and settling lifted the whole cluster; a strongly leaning shard showed its bottom cap.
- Round 2 (review/round2): spikes 25 % thicker with a clearer waist and shoulder; the cluster cut off at
  z = 0 (bisect), so heights are true and no root shows; footprint held to about 2.1 x 1.15 m. Base still
  hidden under the spikes.
- Round 3 (review/round3): a collar of seven slate rocks round the roots. Better, still mostly covered.
- Round 4 (review/round4): collar rocks larger, further out, with more teal lichen (complementary to the
  gold); the dark ring now frames the gold in the game view.
- Round 7 (review/round7, final): base rocks rebuilt with the angular, flat-topped boulders from the rock
  rework; checked together with every other asset in review/round7/lineup_*.png: the clusters read
  instantly as gold minerals at 18, 25 and 40 m.
- Reviewer: my own reading of every render each round, against the brief and the style references.
