# slime_pile — Production Log

## Config
- Type: resource node (Void Slime pile), neutral, game asset for Shardfall (Godot 4.7, Mobile renderer)
- Target: assets/models/slime_pile.glb (uncompressed), assets/icons/slime_pile.png
- Budget: under 2,000 triangles, 3 x 3 m, 0.6-0.9 m tall
- Style: stylised, faceted, readable from the 55 degree game camera at 18-45 m
- Mode: auto (brief delegated 2026-11-14); scripted build, review renders by EEVEE in Blender 5.2

## Reference
- art/ref/void_style_reference.png (the Void's acid-green glow #8CFF3A) and docs/ART_SPEC.md
- Brief: "Void Slime, the second resource. A 3 x 3 m low mound of glossy oily black goo with a purple-green iridescent sheen, a few bubbles and drips, subtle green glow in cracks"

## Intent
- Palette: near-black #0A090E goo; thin-film sheen bands through purple #5B2A8C, blue #2B3F9A,
  teal-green #1E8A6A and green #5FA82A, thin, faint and patchy; fissure glow acid green #8CFF3A (the Void's
  green), base #4FBF2A
- Materials: `slime` (a 256 px sheen texture, roughness 0.08 for a wet gloss), `glow_slime` (emission 0.9,
  so the game can pulse it). Godot imports no iridescence extension, so the sheen is painted into the
  texture.

## Inventory
- A low mound on a 40 x 12 polar grid: a lobed rim (three sine lobes plus four goo tongues spreading out),
  height (1 - rho^2.4)^0.55 for a rounded goo edge, four soft lumps on top; smooth shading
- Six glowing fissures (`glow_slime`): thin ribbons that wander down from near the top, tapering at both ends,
  lifted 12 mm along the surface normal
- Six bubbles (squashed domes sunk into the surface), five droplets on the ground beyond the rim
- UVs: top-down projection over 3.2 m, so the sheen texture runs across mound and bubbles alike

## Pipeline
- Build (art/neutral/build.py, scripted bmesh; lathe, transforms, staging, renders and export loaded from
  art/ambron_vehicles/build.py): parts built with their own UVs and material indices, merged into one mesh.
- Result: 1,736 triangles; 2.622 x 2.837 x 0.825 m (X x Y x Z); lowest point z = 0, centred in X and Y,
  one object `slime_pile` with its origin at the ground centre. Materials: `slime`, `glow_slime`.
- Export: GLB, selection only, modifiers applied, +Y up, no cameras, lights or animations, single-sided
  materials; 101 KB.
- Icon: assets/icons/slime_pile.png, 256 px, transparent, three-quarter from the front-left.

## Review rounds
- Renders in art/neutral/review/round<N>/: front and back three-quarters, the game camera (55 degrees down
  from 25 m, 45 degree vertical field of view, 512 px, on a ground plane) and, in the final round, a sheet; round 7 adds
  lineup_near/game/far.png (every asset together at 18, 25 and 40 m).
- Round 4 (review/round4, first build): Problems: the sheen bands were broad and saturated (psychedelic,
  not oily black); the cracks (inset grid cells, sunk) came out as big blocky zigzags and glowed far too
  bright; 2,036 triangles, over the 2k cap.
- Round 5 (review/round5): sheen bands made thin, faint and patchy on near-black; cracks replaced by
  tapered glowing ribbons along smooth wandering curves, glow toned down (emission 0.9) and base colour
  darkened; 1,736 triangles. Reads as glossy black goo with a purple-green oil sheen and green-lit fissures.
- Round 7 (review/round7, final): unchanged; checked in the lineup: a black pile with green veins, clearly
  the Void's resource next to the gold crystals.
- Reviewer: my own reading of every render each round, against the brief and the style references.
