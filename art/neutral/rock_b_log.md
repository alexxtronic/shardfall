# rock_b — Production Log

## Config
- Type: obstacle (alien rock formation, impassable), neutral, game asset for Shardfall (Godot 4.7, Mobile renderer)
- Target: assets/models/rock_b.glb (uncompressed)
- Budget: under 2,000 triangles, 4 x 2 m, about 1.7 m tall
- Style: stylised, faceted, readable from the 55 degree game camera at 18-45 m
- Mode: auto (brief delegated 2026-11-14); scripted build, review renders by EEVEE in Blender 5.2

## Reference
- docs/ART_SPEC.md (neutral list); the colours come from the brief
- Brief: "alien rock formations used as impassable obstacles. Dark slate-blue/grey stylised faceted rock (#3B4250 to #5A6474) with a little moss-like teal lichen"

## Intent
- Palette: slate gradient by height #2A303A > #353C49 > #454D5C > #5A6474 (and a lighter column up to
  #646E7E), teal lichen #22413B > #3E8577 (light #56A08E) on some up-facing facets
- Material: `rock` only (a 64 x 128 column texture: v follows height over 3 m, u picks slate, light slate or
  lichen per facet), roughness 0.85, flat facets. Principled BSDF only.
- Shape language: crooked basalt-like pillars (stacked 5-7 sided prisms with tilted tops, each segment
  stepping in and shifting along a lean, most ending in a point) among angular flat-topped boulders and
  rubble; each pillar segment keeps one slate tone, so the stacks read as strata.

## Inventory
- a wall of three flattened two-segment pillars (1.25, 1.7, 1.35 m) on a long low base slab, two boulders, three pebbles
- Built at a rough size, then scaled to exactly 3.85 x 1.9 x 1.7 m and centred

## Pipeline
- Build (art/neutral/build.py, scripted bmesh; lathe, transforms, staging, renders and export loaded from
  art/ambron_vehicles/build.py): parts built with their own UVs and material indices, merged into one mesh.
- Result: 238 triangles; 3.85 x 1.9 x 1.7 m (X x Y x Z); lowest point z = 0, centred in X and Y,
  one object `rock_b` with its origin at the ground centre. Materials: `rock`.
- Export: GLB, selection only, modifiers applied, +Y up, no cameras, lights or animations, single-sided
  materials; 23 KB.
- Icon: none (rocks need no icon).

## Review rounds
- Renders in art/neutral/review/round<N>/: front and back three-quarters, the game camera (55 degrees down
  from 25 m, 45 degree vertical field of view, 512 px, on a ground plane) and, in the final round, a sheet; round 7 adds
  lineup_near/game/far.png (every asset together at 18, 25 and 40 m).
- Round 4 (review/round4, first build): Problems: hulls of near-spherical point clouds came out as rounded
  low-poly blobs; pillars read as stacked eggs; facets too few and too large.
- Round 5 (review/round5): more points per hull and a tone per chunk for strata. Still blobby; and the tone
  was read after paint_rock had overwritten it (bug, fixed).
- Round 6 (review/round6): pillar segments rebuilt as irregular 5-7 sided prisms with tilted tops and pointed
  tips, boulders from fewer points with flat mesa tops. Now stylised basalt. New problem: upper segments
  started wider than the ones below (mushroom caps).
- Round 7 (review/round7, final): each segment steps in from the one below; rock_a given a second, leaning
  pillar so it reads as a formation, not a single menhir. Checked in the lineup: slate-blue obstacles with
  teal touches, distinct from both resources.
- Reviewer: my own reading of every render each round, against the brief and the style references.
